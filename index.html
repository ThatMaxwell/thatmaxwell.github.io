XKernel

Your whole machine, as a multi-VM.

Every operating system you use runs side by side as a tile over your wallpaper, on real hardware through Linux's KVM. No bar, no dock, no assistant. Your machines save themselves and resume where you left them. The browser showcase already boots real OSes in tiles.

ChangeOS

The Linux of Android.

Android 17 with nothing you didn't ask for. Two apps preinstalled, Aurora Store and Firefox, plus Termux as the terminal. Google Play is banned. A custom launcher, an accent that the whole system follows, and a status ring that folds battery, signal and Wi-Fi into one shape. Versions are desserts until 7.0, which is just X.

Terminox

A Hyprland-style tiling terminal for Android.

Real Debian, no root. Glass terminal windows that tile themselves (dwindle, master, grid, Niri-style columns), seven themes, live synced lyrics behind your windows, and an AI blob that types commands for you.

Slice

Say it. Slice does it.

An AI-native browser built on Blink with uBlock Origin built in. The new tab is an AI bar: type "yo" and YouTube opens, ask a question and it reads the web and answers with sources, or tell it to make the browser lemon colored.
